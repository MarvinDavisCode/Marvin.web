Old Case Study, Timely Lesson: Fixing Supply Chains Before Scaling

Published September 12, 2026 · Read the original

This post was AI-drafted from published reporting, then reviewed and approved by Marvin before publishing. See "Sources & References" below for the original material this commentary is responding to.

This 2009 Stanford podcast revisits Riders for Health, a Riders for Health, a group that discovered a chunk of Africa's health-delivery motorbikes were sitting broken down over small fixes like $3 oil filters. It's a useful reminder for anyone building mental health programs in resource-constrained settings: the bottleneck to reach isn't always funding or design, sometimes it's basic maintenance and logistics. Worth a quick listen if you're thinking about how care actually gets to people, not just how it's designed.
