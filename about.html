About

Hi, I'm Marvin.

Psychology Implementation Science AI in Mental Health

Mental Health & Behavioral Health Innovation Specialist

Marvin is a mental health and behavioral health professional with a background in psychology and experience spanning nonprofit, government, research, education, and community settings. His work focuses on designing and implementing evidence-based mental health interventions and strengthening the systems that enable people to access effective, timely, and scalable support.

Where his work sits

His work sits at the intersection of psychology, evidence-based interventions, artificial intelligence, implementation science, and human-centered innovation. He has contributed to school- and community-based mental health programs, clinical operations, referral and care pathways, research initiatives, and multidisciplinary program delivery.

On AI and mental health

Marvin is particularly interested in how advances in AI and behavioral science can be responsibly applied to mental health — from prevention and early identification to assessment, learning, triage, referral, and therapeutic support — while preserving human judgment, ethical safeguards, and evidence-based practice.

Training & capacity building

He also works in mental health training and capacity building, supporting practitioners, supervisors, educators, and program teams to translate psychological and clinical knowledge into practical skills and effective implementation.

The bigger picture

His broader professional interest is in building evidence-informed, technology-enabled mental health systems that can expand access, improve quality, and strengthen outcomes, particularly in resource-constrained settings. He is driven by the question of how science, technology, and human-centered care can come together to make mental health support more accessible, effective, and scalable.
