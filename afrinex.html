Afrinex

A weekly publication on evidence-based, technology-enabled mental health care — written for practitioners, educators, researchers, and program teams working to expand access to effective support, including across resource-constrained settings. Published right here every week — join below and I'll reach out directly.

Who this is for

  • Clinicians and practitioners working in mental and behavioral health
  • Educators and school-based support staff
  • Program teams and nonprofit / government staff designing services
  • Researchers in psychology, public health, or implementation science
  • Builders exploring AI and behavioral science applications, responsibly

What you'll get

  • A weekly issue on evidence-based practice and what's actually working
  • Honest takes on where AI helps in mental health care — and where it doesn't
  • Lessons from training, program delivery, and referral systems in the field
  • A personal note from Marvin once you sign up — this is a community, not a list
