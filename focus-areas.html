Focus Areas

Where I spend my professional energy

These are the threads that run through my project work, writing, and training — each grounded in evidence and aimed at expanding access to effective mental health support.

01

Evidence-Based Mental Health Interventions

Designing and implementing interventions grounded in research rather than trend — programs that hold up under real-world conditions across nonprofit, government, and community settings.

02

AI & Behavioral Science Innovation

Exploring how AI and behavioral science can be responsibly applied across the care continuum — prevention, early identification, assessment, learning, triage, referral, and therapeutic support — always with human judgment, ethical safeguards, and evidence-based practice preserved at the center.

03

Systems, Access & Implementation Science

Strengthening the referral and care pathways that determine whether someone actually reaches support — and applying implementation science to close the gap between "the program exists" and "the program works, at scale."

04

Training & Capacity Building

Supporting practitioners, supervisors, educators, and program teams to translate psychological and clinical knowledge into practical, everyday skill — so good ideas turn into good practice.

05

School & Community-Based Programs

Contributing to program design and clinical operations in the settings where people already are — schools and community organizations — rather than requiring people to find their way into the system.

06

Research & Multidisciplinary Program Delivery

Contributing to research initiatives and multidisciplinary teams that connect clinical, technical, and operational perspectives on the same problem — access to timely, effective mental health care, especially in resource-constrained settings.
